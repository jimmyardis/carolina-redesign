The agency's own three percent plan

The same filing includes Form E, which asks every agency how it would absorb a three percent cut in general funds. It is the only point in the budget process where an agency is asked what it would give up. The answer above protects frontline child welfare case managers and eligibility workers and draws on contracts, vacant positions and a furlough.

The number that now has a price

    How this was built, and what it cannot show

    Appropriation figures were read from the ratified acts by script and reconcile to each act's printed department total. Actual spending and performance measures were read from the agency's accountability report. The request figures were transcribed from the agency's budget plan. Program groupings in the ten-year chart follow the act's own headings; the proviso groupings are one analyst's reading.

    The appropriation and the accountability report use the same program names but are separate documents, and the comparison between them is as printed. Nothing here reaches inside a program: staffing by office, contracts, grant balances and cost allocation are not public at that grain.

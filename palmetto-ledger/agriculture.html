How this was built — and what it can’t show

Source one: the Comptroller General’s monthly expenditure transparency files, twelve months of FY2025 — public, free, no records request required. Source two: H.4025, the FY2025-26 General Appropriations Act, Section 44. Every figure on this page traces to one of the two.

Salaries and payroll do not appear in the transaction files; roughly $20.7M of appropriated personnel spending is visible only in the budget section. Categories were assigned by a written, deterministic rule set — the same taxonomy as the statewide Ledger — and about 2.6% of dollars remain unclassified. Actuals are FY2025; the appropriation shown is the adjacent budget year.

The data shows what was bought, not which program it served. Connecting each transaction to a program area requires the Department’s internal cost-center structure — the natural next step for this work.

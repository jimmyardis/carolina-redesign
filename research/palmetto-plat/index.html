Palmetto Plat
The map underneath every town.

A plat is the surveyed map of how land is divided into streets, lots and blocks. Palmetto Plat studies how South Carolina's towns are built and how people move through them — what each acre pays for itself, where the street network connects and where it stops. Everything starts from county parcel records and published budgets, with the sources shown.

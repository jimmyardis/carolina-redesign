The Founder

With a background in government consulting, urban planning, and technology entrepreneurship, Jimmy Ardis brings a rare combination of domain expertise and hands-on AI development experience.

His work for nonprofit and faith-based organizations focuses on operational visibility — helping mission-driven teams see the answers their existing systems already contain. He co-founded the South Carolina Artificial Intelligence Observatory (SCAIO) to track how artificial intelligence is reshaping South Carolina's economy.

Jimmy lives in Chapin, SC with his wife of 23 years, Alisha, and their daughter, Celia.

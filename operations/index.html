If your business runs on clipboards, text messages, and Excel files named "Final_v2", you are leaking revenue.

Every growing organization eventually develops what we call a hidden factory — the unofficial systems, workbooks, and tribal knowledge that keep things running while the official systems lag behind reality.

04 — HOW WE WORK

Three phases. Fixed scope. Outcomes, not hours.

PHASE 01 — THE AUDIT

Process discovery & mapping

We spend a half-day shadowing your team — not just the boss. We map the entire workflow to find the bottlenecks and deliver a written scope-of-work blueprint. Fixed price, fixed timeline, no build begins until the audit is approved.

PHASE 02 — THE BUILD

Custom implementation

We build your custom dashboards and automations. We integrate your existing tools, test workflows, and train your staff on the new digital standard operating procedure. Weekly demos. Documented handoff.

PHASE 03 — THE RETAINER

Fractional CTO posture

Businesses change. We stay on retainer to tweak workflows, fix bugs, and add features as you grow. Think of us as a fractional CTO — present when needed, invisible when not. Optional. Never required.

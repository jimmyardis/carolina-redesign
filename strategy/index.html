Strategic Intelligence · Practice 01

The work that earns the right
to do everything else.

Diagnostic briefings, market analysis, and custom AI built around the questions leadership already wants answered — using only data the organization already owns.

Thesis

The most expensive number in any growing organization is the one nobody can see.

Most consulting engagements end with a deck. Ours end with a working answer — built on real data, delivered as a tool the organization uses every week after we leave.

"We sell outcomes, not hours. Every engagement is scoped to a deliverable the client can describe in a single sentence."

Strategic intelligence is the practice of taking a question leadership has been carrying — about margin, about customers, about offerings, about pipeline — and turning it into a permanent answer. Not a slide. Not a spreadsheet. A system.

What We Build

Three kinds of strategic work.

All delivered the same way — fixed scope, fixed price, working answer.

01 / DIAGNOSTIC

Operational Diagnostics

A consultancy-grade brief examining where an organization's existing systems are obscuring the questions leadership most needs to answer. Mapped pain points, prioritized opportunities, recommended starting wedge. Designed to be read in one sitting.

02 / ANALYSIS

Market Analysis

Sector-specific intelligence that goes beyond what a generalist consultancy can produce — built with AI tooling that reads the entire public surface of a market and surfaces what matters. Delivered as both written report and interactive workspace.

03 / CUSTOM AI

Bespoke AI Tooling

When the question requires a tool that doesn't exist yet, we build it. Budgeting suites, semantic search across institutional knowledge, donor analytics, decision support — narrow-purpose AI sized to a single organizational question.

Methodology

How strategic engagements unfold.

Three phases. Fixed scope. Outcomes, not hours.

PHASE 01 · DAYS 1–10

The Question

A structured listening exercise. We sit with the people who actually maintain the systems and own the question. We deliver a written framing of what's being asked, what's not yet known, and what would be required to answer it.

PHASE 02 · WEEKS 2–6

The Build

We construct the diagnostic, the analysis, or the tool. We integrate with what exists; we replace only what truly needs replacing. Weekly demos. Documented handoff. Staff training included.

PHASE 03 · OPTIONAL

The Continuation

If the diagnostic surfaces additional questions worth pursuing, we scope a follow-on engagement. Most strategic clients eventually move into a retainer relationship — but never as a condition of the initial work.

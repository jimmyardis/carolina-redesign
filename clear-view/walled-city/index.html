About this plate

Clear View is an experiment in pedagogical urban data, modeled on the transparent overlays of mid-century anatomy textbooks. Each layer is one "simplicity" — the public realm, the private realm, the fiscal productivity, the use mix. The "complexity" emerges only when you stack them.

This plate covers the 73.6-acre rectangle that contains the original 1704 enceinte of Charleston, bounded by Meeting Street (west), Cumberland Street (north), East Bay Street (east), and Water Street (south). All 831 parcels are real, drawn from the Charleston County CDC_ParcelMap ArcGIS REST service. The street network is OpenStreetMap, restricted to named highway and alley ways. Property tax is estimated as Appraised × Ratio × 313.6 mills − LOST per the City of Charleston millage card.

The conceptual foundation comes from Leon Krier (Architecture: Choice or Fate, 1998), Jane Jacobs (The Death and Life of Great American Cities, 1961), Walter Kulash (retired traffic engineer who consulted on I'On), and Joe Minicozzi and Chuck Marohn at Urban3 and Strong Towns. The instinct for layered transparency as pedagogy is Vince Graham's — a 2026 email starting a conversation about whether cities could be taught the way bodies are.

Plate II (Elliotborough) and Plate III (I'On) are in preparation. The aspiration is a small archive of plates — each city through the same lens.

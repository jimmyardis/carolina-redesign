About this plate

Clear View is an experiment in pedagogical urban data, modeled on the transparent overlays of mid-century anatomy textbooks and Leon Krier's Res Publica + Res Economica = Civitas. Twelve layers in this plate, each one "simplicity" in Vince Graham's framing: the public realm, the private realm, fiscal productivity, use mix, civic land, ownership, land-vs-improvement, buildings, the 1704 enceinte, the tree canopy, the parking footprint, and the FEMA flood zones. The "complexity" emerges only when you stack them.

The 3D model uses real OSM building footprints with tagged heights for 556 buildings inside the polygon (about 60% of buildings carry explicit height tags; the rest are estimated by use). The "Tax per Acre" layer extrudes each taxable parcel into a column whose height is proportional to its annual property tax revenue per acre — the canonical Urban3 / Minicozzi visualization. All 831 parcels are real, drawn from the Charleston County CDC_ParcelMap. Property tax is estimated parcel-by-parcel per the City of Charleston millage card.

The 1704 enceinte coordinates are approximate, traced from HALS SC-24 and the 1721 Herbert plan. The conceptual framework comes from Leon Krier, Jane Jacobs, Walter Kulash, Joe Minicozzi, and Chuck Marohn. The instinct for layered transparency as pedagogy is Vince Graham's, from a May 2026 email starting a conversation about whether cities could be taught the way bodies are.

Plate II (Elliotborough) and Plate III (I'On) are in preparation.

The Problem

Everyone says "use AI."
Nobody says where.

Generic AI advice fails because it starts with the tools. The assessment starts with your operation — the phone that rings at 7 PM, the invoice that goes out late, the knowledge that lives in one person's head — and only then asks where AI honestly helps.

"The report doesn't tell you what's possible with AI. It tells you what's worth doing in your business, in what order, and what each step pays back."
How It Works

Three steps. One phone call is yours.

Call the assessment line and Reese — our AI interviewer, and it says so upfront — walks your operation end to end: how leads come in, how work gets done, how money gets collected, what eats your week. It asks one question at a time and never pitches you anything.

The Deliverable

What's in your report.

A single interactive page, built entirely from your interview. Here's what it covers.

01 / SNAPSHOT

The Bottom Line, Up Front

The report opens with the headline numbers: how many quick wins we found, the weekly hours they return, what the tools cost per month, and the estimated net monthly return.

02 / MAP

Your Operation, Mapped

Every stage of your operation — from first contact to money in the bank — marked as a leak, a quick win, a bigger play, or running fine. Each one grounded in what you actually told us, quotes included.

03 / SCORECARD

Readiness Scorecard

Six dimensions of AI readiness — from how documented your processes are to how your data is organized — scored in plain English, with an overall maturity stage so recommendations match where you actually are.

04 / QUICK WINS

Six Quick Wins

Six specific, near-term moves, each tied to a stage of your operation and tagged with an implementation path — DIY with instructions, set up with you, or handled entirely. No hundred-item wishlist.

05 / THE MATH

The Math

Hours and dollars, itemized. What each fix saves per week, what it costs to run, and what that nets out to per month — so "worth it" is a number, not a feeling.

06 / SAFE USE

Safe-Use Starter Rules

Starter guardrails for your team, personalized to the sensitive information you actually handle — customer data, payment details, health records, kids' information — so adopting AI doesn't create a liability.

Ground Rules

How the interview is run.

The call works because it's honest about what it is. These rules are built into Reese, not left to chance.

✓

Reese tells you it's an AI, first thing. No pretending to be a person. It asks for your consent to record before the interview starts, and the recording exists so your report can be built from your own words.

✓

It interviews; it doesn't advise. Reese never prescribes tools, prices, or fixes on the call. If you ask "so what should I use?", it will tell you plainly: that's what the report is for — guessing on the phone would get it wrong.

✓

Specifics beat coverage. One real story about the invoice that slipped is worth three vague answers. Reese follows up until it understands how things actually work — not how the brochure says they work.

✓

A human reviews every report. The analysis is AI-assisted; the judgment isn't. Nothing is delivered until a person at Carolina Redesign has read it and signed off.
